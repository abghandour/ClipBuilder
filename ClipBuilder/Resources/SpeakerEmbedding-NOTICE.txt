SpeakerEmbedding.mlpackage

ECAPA-TDNN speaker-embedding model from SpeechBrain
(speechbrain/spkrec-ecapa-voxceleb), converted to Core ML with
scripts/convert_speaker_embedding.py. Input: 16 kHz mono audio; output: a
unit-length 192-dimensional voice embedding. Used on-device to tell the
people in a recording apart by voice.
