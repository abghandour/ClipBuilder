Status: accepted by the user 2026-11-21 with the three recommendations (see Decisions); extended the same day from a reference reel (see Reference reel and the revised phase order); built through phase 8b and committed in 5400c7b (2026-11-23), not released; voice over waiting on the drawing is built but uncommitted. ## What the user asked for

Pause a clip at a moment and draw figures over the picture (arrows, ovals, free-form lines), the way a football broadcast explains a play. In the rendered video the picture freezes there, the figures are drawn on one after another (animated, not all at once), stay for a time the user sets, fade out, and the clip plays on.

## Design

### Model (`Services/Telestration.swift`, pure, Codable, every stored property Optional-safe)

- `Telestration`: `time` (source seconds of the frozen frame), `figures: [Figure]`, `stay` (seconds the finished drawing stays, the number the user sets, default 2.0, range 0.5...10), `drawSpeed` (seconds per figure, default 0.5, range 0.15...2), `fade` (default 0.3).
- `Figure`: `kind` (`arrow`, `oval`, `line`, `free`), `points: [CGPoint]` in 0...1 of the SOURCE frame, top-left origin (arrow and line: start, end; oval: two opposite corners of its box; free: the simplified path), `color` (one of a fixed palette of 5: yellow, red, white, cyan, green), `width` (share of the frame height: thin 0.006, medium 0.010, thick 0.016).
- The freeze lasts `figures.count * drawSpeed + stay + fade`. One number, `Telestration.duration`, used by the editor, the preview and the renderer.
- Limits: 12 figures per pause; a free path is simplified (Douglas-Peucker, tolerance 0.002) and capped at 200 points; at most 8 drawn pauses per clip.
- Source-frame coordinates are the reason crop and 9:16 need no work: the drawing is laid on the source pixels before the crop, as strike highlights are.

### Animation rules (pure, same file)

`Telestration.state(at elapsed:) -> [(figure, progress 0...1)], opacity`:

- Figure `i` draws from `i * drawSpeed` to `(i + 1) * drawSpeed`, eased out; earlier figures are complete, later ones absent.
- Progress is a share of the figure's length: a line or free path is revealed along its arc length; an arrow's shaft is revealed, then its head opens over the last 20%; an oval is traced once round from its top.
- After the last figure, everything stays for `stay`, then opacity falls linearly to 0 over `fade`.

### Painter (`Services/TelestrationPainter.swift`)

One CGContext painter, `draw(_ state:, in:rect:topLeftOrigin:)`, shaped like `StrikeHighlightPainter.draw`: round caps and joins, a dark outline under each stroke (so yellow reads on a pale canvas), arrow head sized from the stroke width. Used by the editor, the previews and the burn-in. No second implementation.

### Editor (`Views/Telestrator/TelestratorSheet.swift`)

One sheet for every entry point, presented by the parent view (never inside a split-view child):

- Top: the frozen source frame, shown inside the clip's output crop with the cropped-away area dimmed, so the user only draws where the viewer will see. Frame step buttons and a scrubber to pick the moment (reuse the stepping in `FightActionPlayback`; extract the player part if needed, do not copy it).
- Tools row (`lineLimit(1)` + `fixedSize`, in a `FlowLayout`): Arrow, Oval, Line, Free line, colour swatches, width, Undo, Clear.
- Drawing: drag to create. A figure can be selected, moved and deleted; no reshaping in the first version.
- Figure list: the order they are drawn in, drag to reorder.
- Timing: "Stays for" seconds, "Drawing speed". The total pause length is shown.
- "Play" runs the animation on the frozen frame with the same rules and painter as the render.
- Save / Cancel. Nothing long runs in the sheet.

Icon: `pencil.tip` is already used for the draw mode in `PreviewPane`; use it for every control that opens this editor.

### Where it is stored and applied

- **Builder.** `TimelineClip` gains `telestration: Telestration?`, meaningful on a hold clip. "Draw on pause" on a selected clip at the playhead: split with the existing `StrikeFreezeRules`/`TimelineSplit` path, insert a hold of `Telestration.duration`, open the editor. Editing the drawing updates the hold's duration. Deleting the hold deletes the drawing.
- **Wizard and Express.** `WizardPlanClip` gains `telestrations: [Telestration]?` (source time, like a strike contact). At assembly `WizardStrikePauses` treats each one as a pause: generalise its pause list from "contacts with one hold length" to "pauses with their own length and an optional drawing"; `Timing` already shifts captions and layers by whatever the pauses are. A drawn pause within 0.25 s of a strike contact replaces that contact's pause (one freeze, not two).
- **Renderer.** A hold with a telestration gets a second overlay stage placed AFTER the frame is cloned (`tpad`) and before the crop: a PNG sequence of the animation at 30 fps for the hold's duration, produced by a `TelestrationBurn` that reuses `StrikeHighlightBurn`'s sequence writer and `inGraph`. The existing strike overlay stays where it is (it is still on the frozen frame).
- **Preview.** Reel preview and Rough cut sheet draw the same state through `ScrubbingPlayerView.pictureOverlay` / the preview column, from the shared clock. The Builder's still preview shows the finished drawing (no animation) on a hold.

### Entry points (every one a visible control)

- Builder: a "Draw on pause" button (`pencil.tip`) in the clip inspector, and on a hold clip "Edit drawing".
- Reel panel (`WizardPreviewList`) and Express Footage list (`RangeReviewView`), in the same pass per the standing rule: a `pencil.tip` button on a clip row opens the editor on that clip; a badge shows how many drawn pauses it has.
- Fight Action popup: "Draw on this action" under the preview, opening the editor at the strike's contact time. Stored on the reel clip that contains that time when there is one; otherwise disabled with a reason.

### Not in this plan

Drawings that follow a player, text labels and numbers, shapes snapping to people, spotlight/dim effects, syncing Builder timelines. (Icon stamps and drawings kept over slow motion were excluded in the first draft and are now phases 2b and 6.)

## Phase 0 findings (2026-11-21, read from the code)

1. **Where a reel clip lives.** Express and Wizard takes are `wizard_selection_takes.plan_json`, written by `Database.updateWizardSelectionTakePlan` through `AppStore.saveWizardTakePlan` / `saveMiniCandidatePlan`. Local, not in Team Sync. `wizard_highlights` is insert-only history and never updated. `WizardPlanClip` decodes with synthesized Codable: `telestrations` must be Optional. So a drawing is local to this Mac in the first version.
2. **Takes are rebuilt from picks.** Manual per-clip data survives a rebuild only where it is captured and re-applied; `brollCuts` does this for transcript picks (`reviewedCutsByPick`, `miniReviewedBRoll`). No such path was found for fight picks or Q&A. Drawings need the same capture and re-apply, for all three kinds, and a trim (`MiniHighlightReview.trimming`, `WizardPlanRules.applyPacing`) must drop a drawing whose time falls outside the clip, as it re-clips `brollCuts`.
3. **Pause lengths.** `Pause` and `Timing` already carry a length per pause. One length is assumed in `timing(for:contacts:seconds:)`, `prepare` (`pauses[0].duration`), `StrikeFreezeRules.pieces(_:contacts:hold:)` and `StrikeClipStatus` (`pauses[0].duration`). `holdRange` is 0.05...5.0 and shared with the Builder and settings; a drawn pause can be longer and needs its own limit.
4. **Gates that block a drawn pause.** `prepare`, `resolveJoins` and `contactPaths` run only when Pause on contact is on and the source is fight footage. `pieces` marks every piece `strikeImpactsFrozen`, and `selection` refuses a clip so marked: strike pauses and drawn pauses must be split in one pass.
5. **Renderer.** A hold is `trim=end_frame=1,tpad=stop_mode=clone` (`MultitrackRenderer.swift` ~2760). A second overlay stage right after it, still in source pixels, is feasible. A hold can span several render segments; the offset into the hold is `timelineOffset - originalStart`. `ResolvedClip` is encoded whole into the segment cache key, so a new stored field is keyed by itself. `StrikeHighlightBurn.prepare` / `inGraph` is the PNG-sequence model to reuse.
6. **Voice over already runs through a hold.** A take is one audio file on the reel clock; `VoiceOverPlacement` already places takes across pauses. Not confirmed: that the mix itself never cuts at a hold. Source audio is muted on a hold (`MultitrackRenderer.swift:1624`), which is what we want.
7. **Stacked and composed layouts.** Other areas are extra video tracks (`areaClips` on track slot+1; composed framing one clip per segment per area). `prepare`, `skipReason` and `pieces` assume one video track. Freezing one area means hold pieces on that track only and every other track's clip lengthened by the hold (its source range runs on).
8. **The Reel preview never plays a hold.** `KeptClipsPlayer` plays the source recording; nothing is drawn over it. Holds, strike highlights and therefore drawings are seen only in the rendered rough cut, where they are baked in. No live overlay is needed in phase 4; the editor's Play is the preview.
9. **Entry points.** `WizardPreviewList` rows have no buttons today (a comment says so on purpose); the Footage list row (`RangeReviewView` ~1040) has a trailing button group. `MiniWizardView` presents the sheets. A row carries `videoURL`, `start`, `end`, `videoID` but not the source aspect or the output crop: `visibleRect` needs a helper over `FramingPlanner.resolve`.
10. **Tests that pin pause behaviour:** WizardStrikePausesTests, StrikeFreezeRulesTests, StrikeClipStatusTests, HoldRenderTests, TimelineClipHoldTests, MultitrackRendererPlanningTests, PreviewWindowTests, WizardBuilderParityTests, VoiceOverTests, WizardRoughCutTests, MiniWizardSettingsTests.

**Consequence: phase 4 is split.**

- **4a. Drawn pauses on a single-source clip.** Storage on the take with capture and re-apply across rebuilds; pauses with their own length, independent of Pause on contact and of the source type; one split pass for contacts and drawings; the renderer overlay stage; the editor opened from the Footage list and Reel panel rows; status lines. Voice over runs through the pause as it already does. Seen in the rough cut.
- **4b. Pauses on stacked and composed layouts** (the reference reel's presenter strip): hold on one track, the others lengthened. Lifts the "composed cut" skip for drawn pauses and Pause on contact alike.

## Phase 4a spec (2026-11-21, accepted: "go ahead with 4a")

**Storage: a drawing belongs to a moment of the footage, not to a reel clip.** This replaces "`WizardPlanClip` gains `telestrations`" above. Reason: takes are rebuilt from picks and trimmed, and only B-roll on transcript picks is carried over today (finding 2). Keyed by video and source time, as strike highlights are, a drawing survives every rebuild and trim by construction and needs no carry-over code. Consequence, accepted for the first version: every reel that contains that moment shows the drawing.

- Table `telestrations(id INTEGER PRIMARY KEY AUTOINCREMENT, video_id INTEGER NOT NULL, at_time REAL NOT NULL, drawing_json TEXT NOT NULL, updated_at REAL NOT NULL)`, index on `video_id`. Local: not in `SyncTable`. Queries in `Data/Database+Telestrations.swift`: fetch for a video, fetch for several videos, save (insert or update by id; `at_time` follows `Telestration.time`), delete. Rows of a deleted video go with it wherever the other per-video rows are removed.
- `AppStore.telestrationRevision` bumped on every save and delete, so anything derived from a reel can tell it is stale, as `strikeHighlightRevision` does.

**4a-1, rules and render (no UI):**

1. `WizardStrikePauses.Pause` gains `drawing: Telestration?`. A new `timing(for:contacts:seconds:drawings:)` builds one ordered pause list per clip: strike contacts (only when Pause on contact is on and the source is fight footage, as today, each with the set hold) and drawings whose `time` lies inside the clip (always, any source type, each lasting `Telestration.duration`, limit 40 s). A drawing within 0.25 s of source time of a contact replaces that contact's pause. A drawing wins a spacing conflict with a contact. The edge and spacing rules (`minimumPiece`) hold for all pauses; the cap of 8 counts contacts only, drawings add at most 8 more. A drawing that cannot be placed is counted and reported per clip: "Drawing: clip N: skipped K (too close to an edge or another pause)."
2. `StrikeFreezeRules.pieces` gets a form taking the pause list (time, hold, drawing) and splits once for all of them; the existing form calls it. The frozen piece carries the drawing: `TimelineClip.telestration: Telestration?` (Optional, synthesized Codable).
3. `WizardStrikePauses.prepare(... drawings: [String: [Telestration]])` keyed by video path like `contacts`. It runs when Pause on contact is requested OR any clip has a drawing; the fight-source gate applies to contacts only. `skipReason` and the dub skip apply to drawings too, with messages starting "Drawing:". Every caller passes the drawings (WizardEngine, WizardPodcastTimeline, MiniStrikeStatus, StrikeClipStatus); the engine fetches them where it fetches contacts. `StrikeClipStatus` no longer reads `pauses[0].duration` as the length of all.
4. Renderer: `ResolvedClip.telestration` copied from the hold clip (it is then part of the segment cache key by itself). In the hold branch, right after the `tpad` stage and before the crop, a `TelestrationBurn` stage lays a 30 fps PNG sequence of the animation on the held frame, starting `timelineOffset - originalStart` into the animation and lasting the segment (a hold can span segments). It reuses `StrikeHighlightBurn.Sequence` and `inGraph`; pictures are at most 1280 wide and are written under the render scratch. A failure renders the hold without the drawing, says so in the log, and clears the cache key, as strike highlights do.
5. Tests: mixed pause lists (order, own lengths, merge with a contact, spacing, limits, messages), `pieces` with mixed pauses (durations, cursor, camera path, the drawing on the right piece), `Timing.outputTime` and captions across mixed pauses, storage round trip, a drawn-hold render compared by timing, audio and per-frame SSIM with the drawing present mid-hold and gone after the fade, a hold split across two segments drawing the continuation, and every pinned pause suite of finding 10 still passing unchanged where behaviour is unchanged.

**4a-2, the controls** (after 4a-1 compiles and is reviewed): a `pencil.tip` menu button on each Footage list row and each Reel panel row, made in the same pass: "New drawing" opens the editor at the row's start, bounded to the row's range, and each existing drawing in the range is listed to edit. A count badge when the row has drawings. `MiniWizardView` presents the sheet. `visibleRect` is passed when the row's output crop is cheaply known, otherwise the whole frame is drawable (a known limitation). Saving bumps `telestrationRevision`; the rough cut is marked stale the way a strike-highlight change marks it.

**Per-reel switch (user, 2026-11-21), part of 4a-2.** `WizardOptions.drawnPauses: Bool?` (nil and true mean on; Optional so saved settings decode). When false, `WizardStrikePauses.prepare` and the status lines take no drawings for that reel: it renders and reports exactly as a reel with none, and the stored drawings are untouched. The control is a "Drawings" toggle beside "Highlight strikes" and "Pause on contact" in the reel's Look settings, in the Wizard and in Express alike, shown wherever those two are shown; its help text says drawings are made from the pencil button on a clip and that this reel can leave them out. Changing it marks the rough cut stale like the other two. Tests: off equals no drawings (cuts, timings, messages), old settings JSON without the key decodes as on.

**Highlight style (user, 2026-11-21), handed over with 4a-2.** The solid limb fill left out of phase 2b. `WizardOptions.strikeHighlightStyle: String?` with `outline` (today's look, the default and what nil means) and `solid` (the limb area filled in red at about 0.75 opacity with a red glow, no green; the red impact ring is drawn as in the outline style, user decision). A "Highlight style" choice (Outline / Solid) sits next to the "Highlight strikes" toggle wherever that toggle is shown, Wizard and Express alike, enabled only while the toggle is on. `StrikeHighlightPainter.draw` takes the style; the render passes the reel's choice down to `StrikeHighlightBurn` and it is part of the segment cache key. Solid needs the HD outline: a strike without one keeps the band it has today, and the help text says so. The Fight Action popup and the Sources inspector keep the outline look. Changing the style marks the rough cut stale. Tests: painter pixel probes for both styles in both coordinate orientations, old settings JSON decodes as outline, the cache key differs between styles.

## Phase 4b spec (2026-11-22; user chose "freeze the whole frame" for both cases)

**Rule.** A pause in a clip that shows several pictures at once (a layout with `areaClips`, or composed framing) is one shared pause: every picture holds at the same reel moment for the pause's length, and the reel gets longer by it. Sound is silent during the hold unless a voice-over runs, as in 4a. Nothing keeps playing, so nothing drifts.

**What can cause a shared pause.** For plan clip `c` with companions `c.areaClips` (each `area.start...min(area.end, area.start + c.end - c.start)`, same speed as `c`):
- contacts and drawings on the main source inside `c.start...c.end` (as 4a);
- drawings on a companion's source inside that companion's shown range, always;
- contacts on a companion's source, when Pause on contact is on and that source is fight footage.
A companion event at source time `t` is at clip-local source offset `t - area.start`; on the main clock that is `c.start + (t - area.start)`. All candidates are merged on the main clock and go through the existing `timing(for:contacts:seconds:drawings:)` rules unchanged (spacing, 0.25 s drawing-replaces-contact, limits, counts). A drawing keeps its own `time` and remembers its owner source: `WizardStrikePauses.Pause.source: String?` and `StrikeFreezeRules.Pause.source: String?` (video path; nil is the main source).

**Splitting.** New `StrikeFreezeRules.sharedPieces(_ tracks: [[TimelineClip]], pauses:) -> [[TimelineClip]]?` (pure). Input is the cut's `videoTrack` grouped by track; pauses are in cut-local screen seconds with lengths. For each pause, on every track, the clip covering that moment is split into head, hold, tail at its own source time for that moment (`sourceStart + local * effectiveSpeed`), with the same camera-path handling as `pieces(_:pauses:)`; later clips on that track move later by the pause. A track with no clip at that moment only shifts its later clips. A pause closer than `minimumPiece` screen seconds to an edge of ANY clip it would split is dropped (counted as a filtered drawing or contact) rather than splitting some tracks and not others. A drawing is attached only to hold pieces whose `videoFile` is the owner source and whose held source time is within one frame (1/30 s) of the drawing's `time`; with composed framing of one source that is every area showing it. Every piece is marked `strikeImpactsFrozen` as in `pieces`. Single-track cuts keep calling `pieces` exactly as today (byte-identical output for 4a reels).

**Gates.** In `skipReason`, the two "composed or B-roll cut needs a shared multi-source pause" returns stop applying to `clip.layout` / `areaClips` and to composed framing. They still apply to cutaways (`isCutaway`, `brollCuts` with B-roll on): reason text becomes "B-roll cutaways would cover the held frame". The "extraction source clock differs" check runs on the track-0 clip(s). Bumper, recipe-transition and dub skips are unchanged. `prepare` fetches drawings and contacts for companion sources too (`drawings(plan:sceneMap:database:)`, `contactPaths`).

**Renderer.** Confirm by test that a hold with and without a drawing renders on tracks above 0 and inside a screen-crop area (drawing lands on the right spot of that area). Fix only what the test shows.

**Status and controls.** `StrikeClipStatus` / `MiniStrikeStatus` stop reporting the composed skip for these clips and count shared pauses once. In the Reel panel (`WizardPreviewList`) a row whose clip has companions lists, in its pencil menu, one "New drawing on <source name>" per companion (range = that companion's shown range) and their existing drawings, labelled with the source name; the Footage list (`RangeReviewView`) gets the same where its row knows the companions, otherwise is unchanged. Both lists changed in the same pass.

**Tests.** `sharedPieces` (two tracks, pause on main, pause from companion, companion shorter than main, edge drop affects all tracks, drawing attached to the right track only, composed same-source attaches to both, clocks and startTimes), `prepare` on a layout clip (timings, messages, no composed skip; cutaway still skipped), a render test with two tracks compared by timing and frames (hold frozen on both areas, drawing visible only in its area, gone after the fade), and proof that single-track output is unchanged.

## Phase 3 spec (2026-11-22): drawings in the Builder

The renderer already draws `TimelineClip.telestration` on a hold (4a). This phase gives the Builder its own controls. A Builder drawing lives on the hold clip in the timeline document; it is independent of the per-video drawings Express stores (an opened reel already carries its own copies).

**Model rules (pure, `Services/TelestrationHoldRules.swift`, tests beside it).**
- `insertion(in clip:at timelineTime:drawing:) -> [TimelineClip]?`: the head, a hold of `drawing.duration` carrying the drawing (its `time` set to the held source time), and the tail, using `StrikeFreezeRules.pieces(_:pauses:)` so source clocks and camera paths match Freeze on impacts. Nil when the clip is a bumper, a cutaway, already a hold, or the moment is closer than `StrikeFreezeRules.minimumPiece` screen seconds to either end, or `drawing.duration` is outside `drawingHoldRange`. Unlike the reel path the pieces are NOT marked `strikeImpactsFrozen` (a hand-made drawing must not block Freeze on impacts on the same clip); a clip that already has the mark keeps it.
- `applying(_ drawing: Telestration?, to hold:) -> TimelineClip`: sets or clears the drawing; with a drawing the hold's duration becomes `drawing.duration`; cleared, the duration is clamped into `StrikeFreezeRules.holdRange`.

**Store (`BuilderStore`).** `drawOnPause(_ uid:at:drawing:) -> Result<UUID, ClipEditFailure>` (returns the hold's uid; one undo step "Draw on Pause"; packing like `freezeOnImpacts` with `resolveLayout`; selection moves to the hold) and `setDrawing(_ uid:drawing:)` (one undo step "Edit Drawing" / "Remove Drawing"; later clips on the track move with the new duration). ## Builder pauses stop the whole timeline (spec, 2026-11-22; user chose "Freeze everything")

Today a pause inserted in the Builder (Draw on pause, Freeze on impacts) lengthens one track only: other video tracks keep playing and titles, overlays, sound cues, B-roll, layout blocks and dub lines stay at their old times. From this change a Builder pause is a pause of the whole timeline, like the whole-frame pause of reels (4b).

**Rule (pure, `Services/TimelinePauseRules.swift`, `nonisolated enum`, tests beside it).**
`insert(_ pauses: [Request], into document: TimelineDocument, resolve: (TimelineClip) -> TimelineClip) -> Result?` where `Request { time (timeline seconds, before any insertion), duration, drawing: Telestration?, owner: UUID }` and `Result { document, holds: [UUID] (the owner track's hold per request, in time order), count }`. `resolve` fills nil `sourceStart`/`sourceEnd` from the scene (the store passes `drawingSource`). Requests are applied in time order; each is refused alone (skipped) when the owner clip fails the existing single-clip rules (`StrikeFreezeRules.minimumPiece` from the owner's ends or from the previous kept pause, duration range, owner is a bumper/cutaway/hold) or the moment lies inside a bumper's span. Nil when nothing is kept. For every kept pause at time `t` with length `d`:
- Every non-bumper clip on ANY video track (cutaways included) that is playing at `t` is split there and gets a hold of `d` on its own frame, through `StrikeFreezeRules.pieces(_:pauses:)` (source clocks, camera path, speed as today). Only the owner's hold carries the drawing. When `t` is closer than `minimumPiece` to such a clip's start or end (other tracks only; the owner is refused instead), the clip is not split: the hold is placed before it showing its first frame, or after it showing its last frame.
- A hold already playing at `t` on another track is not split and not lengthened: the new hold (same frame, no drawing, the new pause's group) is placed directly after it. The picture is the same as lengthening it, and every hold keeps belonging to exactly one pause.
- Every clip and bumper on every track starting at or after `t` moves later by `d`. Free-form and sequential tracks alike; the result must already be packed, so `resolveLayout` afterwards changes nothing.
- Shared lanes move as `BumperPlanner.insertGap` moves them for a pausing bumper: sound cues starting at or after `t` move, one playing across `t` gets longer (music carries on); text and image overlays and overlay blocks starting at or after `t` move, spanning ones stretch; dub lines starting at or after `t` move. Crop (layout) blocks: later ones move, the one spanning `t` stretches by `d` (no full-screen block is inserted, unlike a bumper).
- Dub lines of a split clip are re-pointed: a line starting before `t` to the head piece, otherwise to the tail piece.
- `strikeImpactsFrozen`: unchanged semantics. Freeze on impacts marks the owner's pieces as today; Draw on pause does not; pieces of other tracks keep whatever their clip had.
- All holds made for one pause share a new `TimelineClip.pauseGroup: UUID?` (Optional, nil encodes no key, included in equality and Codable like `hold`). The group's value is the uid of the owner's hold, so the owner is always identifiable: the pause's time is the owner hold's `startTime`. For resizing and removal, on a track that has a hold of the group the clips after that hold move; on tracks without one, and on the shared lanes, everything starting at or after the owner hold's start moves (a hold of the group itself is never moved by its own pause). If the owner hold no longer exists, the remaining holds of that group behave as ungrouped.

**Linked holds.** `resized(group:in:to:)`: every hold of the group takes the new duration and everything later on all tracks and lanes moves by the difference (stretched spans shrink or grow; the same lane rules, mirrored for a negative difference as `BumperPlanner.removeGap` does without dropping items that still have length). `removed(group:in:)`: all holds of the group are removed and the time is closed on all tracks and lanes (the head and tail pieces stay split). Holds without a group (older timelines, reels opened in the Builder) behave exactly as today.

**Store (`BuilderStore`).** `drawOnPause` and `freezeOnImpacts` build requests on the timeline clock and apply the rule as one undo step each (names unchanged; selection as today; `shiftFreeTrack` goes away). `setDrawing` and the Duration trim of a plain hold (`trimClip` on a hold) use `resized` when the hold has a group; deleting a clip that is a grouped hold uses `removed` (one undo step, existing action name). The split-piece rules of `TelestrationHoldRules.applying` stay.

**Out of scope.** Changing how a pausing bumper, cleanup cuts or the reel pipeline work; the renderer; Builder script ops.

**Tests.** Rule: single track equals today's pieces; two tracks both freeze and stay in step after the pause; near-edge clip on another track holds its first/last frame unsplit; free-form track; cutaway covering the moment; bumper after the pause moves, pause inside a bumper refused; each lane (sound spanning and later, text, image, overlay block, crop block spanning and later, dub lines with re-pointing); several pauses at once (Freeze on impacts) accumulate shifts correctly; `pauseGroup` Codable (nil = no key); resize up and down and removal restore lane positions exactly (insert then remove returns the original lane times). Store: one undo step for each of the four edits, redo, old ungrouped hold unchanged behaviour. The existing tests that pin other lanes staying put on insertion (`BuilderDrawingTests.insertionUndo` lines asserting unchanged timed elements, and any Freeze on impacts test doing the same) are expected to change: change only those expectations and list them.

## Phase 6 spec (2026-11-22): figures that stay into the motion

A figure can stay after the pause ends, while the picture moves again. It follows nobody: it is fixed on the source picture, so it keeps its place on screen as long as the framing does not move (as built 2026-11-22: under a moving Center Stage camera or a different crop on the following piece it moves with the picture). It fades out at the end of its stay. Fading everything before the picture moves stays the default.

**Model.** `Telestration.Figure.linger: Double?` (screen seconds after the pause, clamped 0...3 in the initialiser and decoder, nil or 0 = does not stay; nil encodes no key; kept by `normalized`). `Telestration.duration` is unchanged: the pause is as long as before.

**Animation rules (pure, `TelestrationRules`).** `FigureState` gains `opacity: Double` (default 1) and the painter fades each figure by `state.opacity * figure.opacity` (figures of equal opacity may share a transparency layer; the result for drawings without lingering figures must be pixel-identical to today, the existing painter tests stay green unchanged).
- During the pause (`state(of:at:)`): as today, except that a lingering figure keeps opacity 1 while the others fade over `fade`.
- After the pause: new `lingerState(of:at elapsed:) -> State` (seconds since the pause ended): only figures with `linger > 0`, fully drawn, opacity 1 until `linger - min(fade, linger)`, then a linear fade to 0 at `linger`; empty when nothing is left. `lingerDuration(of:) -> Double` = the longest linger.

**Render.** No document change: at resolve time (`MultitrackRenderer`, where a hold's `telestration` is attached to its resolved clip) the clip that follows a drawn hold on the same track, with the same source file, starting where the hold ends (within 1/60 s) and not itself a hold, bumper or cutaway, gets the hold's drawing as a transient `lingering: Telestration` (not Codable, never stored) when `lingerDuration > 0`. It is burned on that clip's source picture before framing, like the strike highlight on a moving clip (`StrikeHighlightBurn.inGraph`, PNG sequence from `TelestrationBurn`), for `min(lingerDuration, the clip's screen duration)` screen seconds; the animation clock is screen time, so on a clip with speed != 1 the pictures are spaced by the clip's speed in source time. A preview window or segment that starts part-way through that clip starts the animation at the matching moment (same idea as `Telestration.offset` on a hold; carry it the same way in `windowed`). A following clip shorter than the stay ends it there; it never carries over to a third clip. A failure to prepare the pictures degrades like a failed drawing does today (emit a line, render without, segment cache key cleared). The segment cache key of the following clip's segment must change when the lingering figures change. Applies to reels and the Builder alike, since both render through holds.

**Editor.** With a figure selected, the style row gets "Stays after the pause" (a slider 0...3 s, step 0.1, with the value shown; `lineLimit(1)` + `fixedSize`; help: "Keeps this figure on screen while the picture moves again"). A new figure takes 0. Play in the editor continues past the pause for `lingerDuration` showing only the staying figures, with the caption "the picture moves here" under the picture during that part. The timing row is unchanged.

**Not in this phase.** Figures that follow a fighter; a stay longer than 3 s or across clips; showing staying figures in the Builder's live preview (the exact preview and the render show them).

**Tests.** Rules: state during the pause with mixed figures; `lingerState` at 0, mid, in the fade, at the end; clamp and Codable (nil = no key, old JSON decodes). Painter: a drawing without lingering figures is unchanged (existing tests); a mixed drawing at the last pause frame shows the staying figure at full strength and the other faded. Burn: frame times for speed 1, 0.5 and 2 and for a window starting part-way. ## Phase 6b spec (2026-11-22): a staying figure is fixed on the screen

## Center Stage continuity across a pause (spec, 2026-11-22)

User: "fix the center stage framing jump too". Seen live in the Builder: a Center Stage clip split by a pause (head, hold, tail) jumps at the pause's start and end, because the renderer tracks people separately for each piece (the hold over 1/30 s) and each piece's camera starts from its own first look. Reel clips that carry a stored `cameraPath` do not have this (a split slices the path).

**Rule.** Pieces that are one continuous take are framed by ONE camera path. A run is a maximal sequence of clips on the same track that follow each other on the timeline (next start = previous end within 1/60 s), have the same source file, speed-independent identical framing inputs (Center Stage on, same layout area / region / focus, same framing plan and camera tuning, same VIP keys), are not bumpers or cutaways, and whose source ranges continue: a moving piece starts where the previous moving piece ended (within 1/60 s of source time), and a hold shows a frame at the join (its `sourceStart` within 1/60 s of the previous piece's source end or the next piece's source start). A layout (crop block) change inside the run ends it.
- For a run of two or more pieces whose camera would be tracked fresh (no stored path that applies), the renderer tracks once over the union source range, with the same tuning and inputs a single unsplit clip would get, and gives each piece its slice of that path on its own clock: a moving piece the keyframes of its source range (interpolated at both ends), a hold the path's value at its frame held for its duration. Each piece is then rendered through the existing stored-path route. The picture in the last frame before a pause, during the pause and in the first frame after it is framed identically (within 1 px).
- Runs of one piece, pieces with an applicable stored path, static framing: exactly today's behaviour, graph strings and cache keys.
- Cost: tracking the union once replaces tracking each piece, never more analysis than today. The run's path is cached under a key of the union (source, range, framing inputs, tuning), so editing a pause's length does not re-track.
- Where the stored-path route cannot reproduce what fresh tracking renders for that framing (for example a tuning that stabilises camera motion in the export, or a fallback when tracking finds nobody), the run keeps today's per-piece behaviour and the log says "Center Stage: pieces framed separately (<reason>)".
- Applies wherever the renderer resolves clips (Builder renders, exact previews, reels). A windowed preview that shows only part of a run still uses the whole run's path, so preview and export agree.
- A staying figure's window (phase 6b) comes from the hold's slice of the run path.

## Dub pauses, second design (2026-11-22): the silence is derived from the freezes

The review of the first implementation found five ways the stored gap (`DubLine.pauses`, a position inside the line's audio) goes stale while the freeze moves: render-time joins that consume time between the line's start and the gap (`RenderClock.swift:56`, `RenderEngine.swift:709`), "Prepare again" building fresh lines (`DubPreparation.swift:191`, `DubPlanning.swift:39/134`), trimming and speed changes that repack clips (`BuilderStore.swift:1714/2084/2188`), cleanup cuts (`TimelineModels.swift:441`), bumper insertion and removal (`BumperPlanner.swift:170`). Storing the gap is the wrong design. What the user hears does not change ("Stop and resume").

**Design.** Nothing about the gap is stored on the line. `DubLine.pauses` and everything that writes it (in `TimelinePauseRules`, `DubPauseRules`, preparation/synthesis clamping) is removed; an old document that has the key decodes and ignores it (the field never shipped).
- **Freezes** of a document = the time intervals of whole-timeline pauses: hold clips with a `pauseGroup` (the union over the group's members, merged when they touch), in the clock in use. One pure function `DubFreezes.intervals(in document:)` (timeline clock) and a variant that maps them through the render clock the mixer uses (`RenderClock`: joins, bumpers, recipes, timed assembly), so the silence is computed in the same clock as the picture it sits under.
- **A line's layout** = pure function of (start, audible duration, freezes): the line plays from its start; at each freeze that begins while the line is still speaking it stops, and resumes when the freeze ends; a line whose start falls inside a freeze begins when it ends. Result: audible segments (offset in audio, start in time, length) and the line's end. `timelineDuration` becomes this derived end minus start, computed with the document's freezes (callers that have no document at hand must be given the freezes; no stored fallback).
- **Edits need no dub bookkeeping**: inserting, moving, resizing, deleting a pause, trims, speed changes, cleanup cuts, bumpers all just change the holds; the next layout follows. `TimelinePauseRules` keeps moving line STARTS exactly as it did before dub pauses existed (lines starting at or after the pause move; the 20 ms edge rule added by the first implementation is reverted to the original comparison).
- **Planning ("Prepare", "Prepare again", slot fitting, overlap checks, tight marking)** works on the motion clock: timeline time with the freezes removed. A line is planned and fitted there (a freeze is neither free time for a line to run into nor an obstacle), then placed back on the timeline. A line never gains slot time from a freeze.
- **Mixing** (export, exact preview): each line is cut into its audible segments on the render clock with 10 ms voice fades at each cut. Ducking of the original sound follows the audible segments; its ramps at a freeze boundary are 40 ms and must be sample-accurate in effect: the `volume` expression is evaluated per audio frame, so make frames short enough before it (e.g. `asetnsamples` 256) only on paths that have a freeze inside a line, or use an equivalent that cannot skip a ramp. Balanced expression trees as before.
- **Cache**: the dub audio cache key includes each line's derived segments (so moving a freeze re-mixes) and nothing for lines that meet no freeze (byte-identical graph strings, keys and JSON to before dub pauses for those).
- **UI**: the `DubInspector` row ("N line(s) stop for a freeze") and the `DubReviewLineRow` label are computed from the same layout function.
- Reel timelines: holds made by reel pauses have no `pauseGroup` and dubbed reels skip pauses: unchanged.

**Tests.** Pure: freezes from holds (groups merged, non-group holds ignored); layout (no freeze; one inside; two inside; freeze at the line's first and last instant; start inside a freeze; freeze after the line); motion-clock planning (a hold after a planned line gives no extra slot; a line across a hold is fitted to its motion time). Document edits each followed by layout: trim the piece before the pause, change its speed, cleanup cut before the pause, insert and remove a bumper before the pause, delete the hold, undo each: the silent interval equals the hold's interval every time. Render (integration, decoded audio and video together): build the document through `TimelinePauseRules.insert`, render, find the frozen video frames and the silent audio window: they coincide within 1 frame, also with a crossfade join and with a bumper between the line's start and the pause; samples after the freeze equal the unpaused render shifted by the pause. Lines meeting no freeze: same filter string as before.

### 7a spec

**Rules (pure, `Services/StrikeImpactRules.swift`, `nonisolated enum`, tests beside it).**
- `Strength: String, Codable, CaseIterable { subtle, medium, strong }` with `lead` (source seconds before contact) and `factor` (speed multiplier): subtle 0.5 s / 0.6, medium 0.5 s / 0.4, strong 0.6 s / 0.25; labels "Subtle", "Medium", "Strong". `flashDuration = 0.12`.
- `slowed(_ pieces: [TimelineClip], contacts: [Double], strength:) -> [TimelineClip]`: `pieces` are one clip's pieces in order (after pauses, so they may contain holds); `contacts` are source seconds. For every moving piece (not hold, bumper, cutaway) and every contact `c` with `pieceStart < c <= pieceEnd` (a contact at the piece's end is the usual case after a pause split): the stretch `[c - lead, c]` clamped to the piece's start becomes its own piece at `speed * factor` (duration = source length / new speed, source range unchanged, camera path sliced as `TimelineSplit` does), provided every resulting piece is at least `StrikeFreezeRules.minimumPiece` screen seconds; a contact whose stretch would overlap the previous contact's stretch or fail the minimum is skipped and counted. A contact strictly inside a piece also splits the piece at `c`. Later pieces move later by the added screen time. ### 8b spec: the reel plays the picture at the drawing's speed

Until 8b a drawing with `motion` renders as a pause (the pipeline ignores the key). 8b makes Wizard and Express reels render it; everything that cannot is the pause it is today.

**Rule.** A moving drawing at source time `t` on a clip of speed `s`: the picture plays on from `t` at `s * motion` for `drawing.duration` screen seconds (source span `duration * motion * s`), the figures are drawn over it by the usual animation clock (`TelestrationRules.state`), then the clip continues at `s`; figures with `linger` stay into what follows as they do after a pause. No frame is held. `motion == 1` adds no time and still carries the drawing.

**Falls back to a pause** (the drawing with `motion` cleared, through today's path, one message per clip "Drawing: clip N: the picture pauses (reason)"): the clip shows several pictures (shared cut, companions, composed layout); the source span does not fit before the clip's end minus `minimumPiece` screen seconds; any resulting piece is under `minimumPiece`; the resulting speed is under `StrikeImpactRules` minimum speed (0.25); the span overlaps another kept drawing or a kept contact pause. Dubbed reels and the other `skipReason` cases skip it as they skip a drawn pause. The Builder is out of scope (its holds stay pauses).

**Pure rules** (tests beside them):
- `TelestrationRules.carriedDuration(of:)` and `carriedState(of:at:)`: for `motion == nil` exactly `lingerDuration` and `lingerState` (pin with a test); for a moving drawing the clock starts at the drawing's start: `state(of:at:)` for `elapsed < duration`, then `lingerState(elapsed - duration)`; duration `duration + lingerDuration`.
- New `nonisolated enum TelestrationMotionRules` (`Services/TelestrationMotionRules.swift`), modelled on `StrikeImpactRules.slowing`: `moving(_ pieces: [TimelineClip], drawings: [Telestration]) -> (pieces, stretches: [StrikeImpactRules.Stretch], fallback: [Telestration])`. `pieces` are one clip's pieces after pauses. For each drawing inside a moving piece: split at `t` and at `t + span` (`StrikeImpactRules.slice` / `TimelineSplit` for the camera path), the middle piece gets `speed * motion`, `duration = drawing.duration`, `telestration = drawing`, and the same lineage marking slow pieces get (`slowLineage` / `stayCut == false` on the automatic splits) so a carried drawing continues across them; later pieces move later by the added time.
- `WizardStrikePauses.timing(for:contacts:seconds:drawings:)`: a moving drawing that passes the checks is not a `Pause`; a contact within 0.25 s of its start or inside its span is replaced by it (not counted as skipped), as a drawn pause replaces one. `preparePauses` applies `TelestrationMotionRules` after the pause pieces, adds the stretches to `Timing.stretches` on the planned clock (as the single-picture slow branch of `prepare` does), then `mapLayers`; `prepare`'s slow-into-contact skips a contact whose stretch would overlap a moving drawing's (counted in `filteredSlowContacts`).
