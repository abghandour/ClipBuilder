In one paragraph. The fight-action editor, the branding setup, the person-centered screen layouts, the data bank, the Instagram Reel path, and the Instagram half of the intelligence layer are built and in daily use. The document's largest asks that do not exist yet are the long-form podcast editor (multi-camera, Zoom feed, chaptering, dead-air cuts), everything TikTok and YouTube (output, metrics, publishing), selectable output sizes (the app renders 1080×1920 only), translated captions, and the retention-based hook and cut analysis. Auto-posting, spreadsheet output, and the Windsor / HypeAuditor / OpusClip connections are also absent.

5. Where the effort is

Ranked by size of the gap, largest first.

  • Long-form podcast editor. Multi-camera ingest and sync, Zoom feed, chaptering, dead-air detection, and a horizontal YouTube render. Nothing exists today; this is a new product surface.
  • Multi-platform output and metrics. TikTok and YouTube publishing and insights, plus carousel, feed, and story creation for Instagram. The Instagram provider architecture is a template for the others.
  • Output sizes. Parameterizing the 1080×1920 canvas through layouts, overlays, framing, captions, and both renderers. Mechanical but touches many files.
  • Retention-based analysis. Hook retention, drop-off points, cut timing versus retention, screen-type performance, and athlete return. Depends on per-second retention data the platforms expose only partially.
  • Automation and integrations. Scheduling and auto-post with approval, spreadsheet output, and the Windsor, HypeAuditor, and OpusClip connections.
  • Small items. Translated captions, an explicit "every N seconds" cadence control, lower thirds as a template, B-roll tagging with photo suggestions, topic separation.
